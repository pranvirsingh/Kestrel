# Changelog

Every release of Kestrel, newest first. Download any version from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/pranvirsingh/Kestrel/releases).

## v2.0.0 — 2026-10-02

The first public release. Version 2.0 of the game; version 1.0 was never
published.

### New
- A campaign of 6 sectors (Coral Coast to Stratos), each with its own map, boss and ace challenge
- 4 threat levels per sector, from Recon to Nightmare, unlocked by earning badges
- 96 badges: Sweep, Lifeline, Untouched and Ace for every sector and threat level
- Hangar upgrades bought with cores, and 10 permanent pilot-rank perks
- Every aircraft, tank, boat, turret and boss is a 3D model, rendered once (units on first launch, each boss on its first mission) and then cached
- Raised terrain with lighting, soft shadows and shaded cliff faces
- Overdrive: double-tap or press the prism to slow time and turn enemy rounds into cores
- Synthesised music and sound effects, with sound, music, vibration and sensitivity settings
